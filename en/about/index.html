About Libria

Libria is a bilingual virtual library for discovering books by literary world, reading a concise editorial note, consulting external criticism and finding an edition to buy.

Editorial method

Each page separates the work summary, Libria’s editorial note and external criticism. External sources are named and linked.

Source selection

We favour literary blogs, reviews and reading publications that discuss the specific book.

Covers and metadata

Covers mainly come from Open Library, with Google Books as a fallback. Language editions are kept distinct from the work.

Commerce

Editorial recommendations remain separate from merchant links. Affiliate links are explicitly labelled.
